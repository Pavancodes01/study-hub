Back-of-envelope

Estimate in two minutes

You need the order of magnitude, not the exact number. Round hard, say each step out loud, and finish with what the numbers mean for the design.

Trainer

A random scenario, one question at a time. Answers within 2× of the exact value count as right. Write units like 40, 4k, 350M, 3 TB or 2e9.

The method

  1. Write your assumptions

    Daily users, actions per user per day, size of one item, how long you keep data, read:write ratio.

  2. Requests per second

    Per day ÷ 100,000 (a day is 86,400 s; round to 10⁵). Peak ≈ 2–3× average.

  3. Storage

    Items per day × size × 365 × years. Multiply by 3 if you count replicas.

  4. Bandwidth

    Requests per second × size of each response.

  5. Say what it means

    "4k reads/s fits a few cache nodes; 3 TB fits one sharded KV cluster; the hard part is X."

Say it in the interview

  • Let me round a day to 100,000 seconds.
  • 100 million a day is about 1,000 per second, so peak is around 3,000.
  • So writes are small, but storage grows to about 3 TB in five years.
  • That tells me the hard part is reads, not writes.
