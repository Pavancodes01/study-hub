How to study one structure

About half an hour each. The goal is to see the memory picture whenever you type the Java.

  1. Play firstUse the playground at the top. Push it until something interesting happens: a resize, a collision, an eviction.
  2. Step the demoIn "How it works", step through the mechanism and say why each step costs what it costs.
  3. Learn the costsRead the operations table. Remember the reason column, not just the Big-O.
  4. Run the JavaCopy a snippet into your IDE, change it, predict the output before running.
  5. Build it, drill itType the "Build it yourself" class from memory, then pass the drills and solve the listed problems.
